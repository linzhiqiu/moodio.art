01 核心壁垒01 Why We Win

创意对齐做不好、工作流被切成碎片、创意资产无处沉淀——这三件事专业团队烧了几十年，没人真正解过。原因很简单：现在的工具都是给个人创作者做的。Moodio 从一开始就为专业影视团队而生。 Creative alignment falls apart, the workflow lives in pieces, and the work itself never accumulates — three problems studios have burned on for decades, never properly solved. The reason is simple: every existing tool was built for individual creators. Moodio was built for professional film teams from day one.

The right one — still or video — instantly aligns the team and the client, and communication cost drops on the spot. On our publicly released cinematic retrieval benchmark Moodio-T2V, Moodio outperforms both Google Gemini Embedding 2 and Alibaba's Qwen-3-VL Embedding.

导演脑子里有什么镜头，嘴上说出来就行。"焦点从前景转到背景的变焦"、"从昏暗室内推到明亮室外"——这种有镜头变化的专业描述，只有 Moodio 能搜到。Whatever shot the director has in mind, they just say it out loud. "A rack focus from a foreground subject to the background street," "a push from a dim interior out into bright exterior" — only Moodio can retrieve descriptions like these.

2 对应痛点 · 工作流碎片化Pain · Fragmented Workflows
工作流智能 · 自然语言协同管理Workflow Intelligence · Natural-Language Team Coordination

影视、广告、动画的工作流从来就不是单线的——摄影、剪辑、美术、导演、分镜师并行作业，制片人在中间穿针引线。Agent 接管的就是这个穿针引线的活。Film, advertising, and animation have never been single-track workflows — DPs, editors, art leads, directors, and storyboard artists all run in parallel, with a producer threading the needle in the middle. The agent takes over that threading-the-needle job.

"帮我把这几张放到一个集合里"、"帮我找到汪导上周做的那个空境镜头"、"帮我把这批素材按场景归类"——一句话讲清楚要做什么，Agent 就去做。原本靠鼠标点击的协作，全换成对话。整支团队的协作方式，自然就变了。"Group these frames into a collection." "Find that empty-frame shot Director Wang did last week." "Sort these clips by scene." Say what you want in one sentence and the agent does it. What used to be mouse-clicks turns into conversation, and the way the whole team works together changes.

从 1. 搜参考、2. 写脚本、3. 出分镜表、4. 生成画面、5. 生成视频、到 6. 剪辑导出——Agent 嵌在画布的每个环节里。导演、摄影、美术、视频团队站在同一块画布上看同一份方案。甲方反馈从"感觉不对"变成"这个方向换一下"。From 1. searching references, 2. writing scripts, 3. building shot lists, 4. generating frames, 5. generating video, to 6. editing and exporting — the agent is embedded in every step of the canvas. Director, DP, art lead, and the video team all stand on the same canvas, working from the same brief. Client feedback shifts from "something feels off" to "change this direction."

3 对应痛点 · 创意资产沉淀Pain · Creative Asset Accumulation
个性化审美智能 · 24 小时懂你的视觉助理Personalized Aesthetic Intelligence · A Visual Assistant That Knows You

AIGC 影视有两个绕不开的痛点。第一，要做一个 AIGC 项目，需要一个人同时懂表演、摄影、美术、画道、剧本——这种全能的人几乎找不到，找到也很难复制。第二，我自己知道这张图想怎么用，但传给下游的人未必知道——协作里有大量信息损耗。AIGC filmmaking has two stubborn problems. First, shipping an AI-driven project takes someone who understands acting, cinematography, art direction, drawing, and screenwriting all at once — that polymath is almost impossible to find, and even harder to replicate. Second, you know exactly how you want that frame to be used, but the person downstream often doesn't — and a lot of intent gets lost in handoff.

个性化审美智能从根本上解决这两件事。Agent 学的，是你和团队每天做出的视觉决策：用过哪些风格、选过哪些方向、最后留下哪些。这些数据攒成一个 24 小时懂你的视觉助理——你可以理解成专做视觉创意的 Manus。Personalized aesthetic intelligence solves both of these at the root. The agent learns from the visual decisions you and your team make every day — which styles you tried, which directions you picked, what you kept. That data builds up into a visual assistant that knows you around the clock — think Manus, but for visual creative work.

从个人风格到团队审美再到跨项目复利——三层递进。一个人用，Agent 懂他的偏好；一支团队用，Agent 摸清整个工作室的视觉语言和品质底线；跨多个项目用，Agent 已经看过你们怎么取景、怎么打光、怎么剪每一个镜头，第十一集可以按你们的习惯先把初稿做出来。From personal style to team taste to cross-project compounding — three layers. One person uses it, the agent learns their preferences. A team uses it, the agent learns the studio's visual language and quality bar. Across many projects, the agent has watched how you frame, light, and cut every shot — by episode eleven, it can draft a first cut the way you would.

越用越值钱Gets More Valuable Over Time
团队的创意决策都在 Moodio 里持续累积。每多做一个项目，Agent 就更懂你们一分，下一个项目从更高的起点开始。

这是一份会复利的资产。新人加入工作室，Agent 直接把团队的审美和品质底线教给他，原本要几周磨合的事，几天就能跟上节奏。 A team's creative decisions keep building up inside Moodio. Every project you ship, the agent learns you a little better — and the next project starts from a higher floor.

This is an asset that compounds. New hires get onboarded straight to the team's aesthetic and quality bar through the agent — what used to take weeks of acclimation now takes hours.

市场在验证这个方向，

市面上想做的人不少，但没有一家把三件事同时做对：电影级检索、有记忆的 Agent、专业团队协作。竞品的存在恰恰说明这条路有需求——只是没人跑到终点。 Plenty of teams are trying. None has gotten all three right at once: cinematic retrieval, an agent with memory, professional team collaboration. The competitors prove the demand — they just haven't reached the finish line.

为什么没人做对WHY NO ONE HAS GOTTEN IT RIGHT

最像 Moodio 的两类对手：一类是自媒体出身的 Agent 工具（TapNow），画布撑不住大项目，检索只能做关键词匹配，Agent 没记忆。另一类是消费级创作工具（即梦 Octo），完成度高但定位是 vibe creator，没有行业级素材库，也没为专业团队做工作流。 The two closest competitors: one is an agent tool with roots in short-form media (TapNow) — the canvas can't hold a real project, retrieval is keyword-matching only, the agent has no memory. The other is consumer creative tooling (Dreamina Octo) — polished but built for vibe creators, no industry-grade library, no workflow built for professional teams.

两类都解决不了专业影视团队的真问题，因为从第一天起就不是为这群人做的。Moodio 不一样：研究和创始团队从一开始就在做专业影视——CameraBench、CHAI、VQAScore、GenAI-Bench，每一篇研究都对应影视团队天天用到的东西：运镜、构图、光线、画面质量。谁在做、为谁做，决定了最后能做出什么。 Neither solves the real problem for professional film teams — because from day one, neither was built for this audience. Moodio is. Our research and founding team have been on professional film from the start. CameraBench, CHAI, VQAScore, GenAI-Bench — each paper maps to what film teams actually use every day: camera work, composition, lighting, image quality. Who builds it, and who they build it for, decides what gets built.
